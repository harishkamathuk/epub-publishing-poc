# Chapter 3 — The Decision

By evening, the clues had formed a pattern that could no longer be ignored. Each discovery pointed toward the same conclusion, even if none of them explained why events had unfolded this way.

Standing above the valley, Maya understood that returning home would be the easier choice. Staying meant accepting uncertainty, responsibility, and the possibility that everything she believed would change.

She took one last look across the landscape before making her decision. Whatever happened next, this was only the beginning of the story.
