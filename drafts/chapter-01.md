# Chapter 1 — The Arrival

The train reached the station just after sunrise, carrying far fewer passengers than anyone had expected. The platforms were unusually quiet, and every sound seemed to echo through the cool morning air.

Maya paused before leaving the carriage. She had travelled a long way to reach a town she had never seen, guided only by a letter written months earlier. Nothing about the place matched the picture she had imagined.

As she stepped onto the platform, she noticed a single figure waiting beneath the station clock. Whether they had come to welcome her or warn her, she could not yet tell.
