# Chapter 2 — The Library

The old library stood at the edge of the town square, hidden behind rows of chestnut trees. Its stone walls carried the marks of centuries, while its shelves seemed to stretch endlessly into the shadows.

Unlike the station, the library was full of quiet activity. Readers whispered between towering bookcases, librarians moved with practiced precision, and somewhere in the distance an old clock marked the passing of each hour.

Maya quickly realised that the building contained more than books. Every visitor appeared to be searching for something different, and every answer seemed to lead to another question.
